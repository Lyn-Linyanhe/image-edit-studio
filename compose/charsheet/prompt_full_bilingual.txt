STYLE TO APPLY / 要施加的画风 - transferred from the style reference image / 从风格参考图迁移
Anime digital illustration with painterly cel shading. Expressive variable-width linework with tapered brushstrokes: thin clean near-black contours that thicken where forms overlap and taper to sharp points at hair tips and cloth edges. Shading built from graphic flat shadow shapes with crisp boundaries and only short transition zones. Selective warm highlights in warm ivory on lit edges of hair, cheek and dress. Subtle directional brush texture inside large flat colour areas. Selective fine detailing concentrated at the eyes, jewellery and flower ornament, sparser inside large filled areas. Lively and delicate mood. Clean shapes, crisp edges, consistent colour grading, high clarity, coherent lighting.
动画数字插画，绘画式分区上色。富有表现力的变化粗细线稿与收尖笔触：细而干净的近黑轮廓线，形体交叠处加粗，发梢与布边收成尖点。明暗由图形化平涂暗部块面构成，边界清晰，只有很短过渡带。在头发、脸颊与裙子的受光边缘施加暖象牙色选择性暖高光。大面积平涂色块内部带顺形细短笔痕。细节密度有选择性：集中在眼睛、首饰与花朵发饰，大面积填色区内部更疏。情绪轻快而精致。形体干净、边缘清晰、色调统一、清晰度高、光照一致。
